---
title: Staff roles and what they can do
lead: What distribution staff can do in 121 Scan follows their role in 121 for the program.
---
- Staff log in with their own 121 account. 121 Scan has no separate passwords.
- **Without permission to update payments**, the send section does not appear, and the beneficiary screen is for checking only. It shows *{{ app.verify_only_note }}* and has no **{{ app.payment_approved }}** button.
- **Without permission to update registrations**, editable fields are not shown.
- Staff see every program listed in **{{ app.config_system }}**.

To change what someone can do, change their role in 121. The change applies the next time they open the program screen.
